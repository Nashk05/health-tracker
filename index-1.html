Period calendar

Period Predicted period Fertile window Ovulation (estimate) Energy and social days PMS-prone days Lower-chance days

Lower-chance days only show after you have logged 3 periods and your cycle is fairly steady.

Tap a day to log it and tap again to remove it. Filled days are logged and dashed days are predicted.

Fertile and lower-chance days are only estimates, and no day is completely safe, so do not rely on this app to prevent pregnancy. See a doctor if your periods stop for several months, are very painful or very heavy, or are very irregular. Entries stay in this browser.

Last 7 days

Many people aim for 7,000 to 10,000 steps a day. Teal bars mean you reached your goal.

In the Android app, steps sync from your phone’s step sensor when you open the app and every minute while it is open. You can also type your steps. Distance and calories are rough estimates.

Reminders

If reminders do not arrive on a Samsung phone, open Settings, Apps, Health Tracker, Battery and choose Unrestricted.

Backup your data

Your entries are saved only inside this app, and uninstalling the app deletes them. Save a backup file now and then, and keep it in Google Drive or send it to yourself.
